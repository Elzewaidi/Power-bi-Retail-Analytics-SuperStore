## 🎯 Project Overview
This project focuses on transforming raw retail data into actionable business intelligence. The dashboard provides stakeholders with deep visibility into overall sales performance, profit margins, shipping modes, product categories, and regional performance to support data-driven decision-making.

---

## ✨ Key Features & Business Questions Answered
- **Executive Summary / Overview:** High-level KPIs including Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Quantity Sold, and Profit Margin.
- **Sales & Profitability Analysis:** Breakdown of performance by Product Category, Sub-Category, and Customer Segment.
- **Geographical Insights:** Regional and state-level performance mapping to identify top-performing markets and underperforming areas.
- **Time-Series Trends:** Monthly and yearly sales growth trends to spot seasonality and peak shopping periods.
- **Customer & Shipping Insights:** Analysis of customer purchasing behavior and shipping mode preferences.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- **Microsoft Power BI:** Data visualization, interactive filtering, and dashboard design.
- **Power Query:** Data extraction, transformation, and cleaning (ETL process).
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Calculated columns, measures, and key business metrics.
- **Excel / CSV:** Source data management and preprocessing.

---

## 📈 Key Insights & Findings
- **Top Categories:** Technology and Office Supplies drive the majority of sales, while Furniture shows lower profit margins relative to sales volume.
- **Regional Performance:** The West and East regions dominate in terms of total revenue and profitability.
- **Shipping Modes:** Standard Class is the most utilized shipping method among customers, followed by Second Class and First Class.

---

## 🚀 How to View / Use
1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open the `.pbix` file using **Microsoft Power BI Desktop**.
3. Interact with the slicers, drill-throughs, and cross-filtering features to explore the data.
